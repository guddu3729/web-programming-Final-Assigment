Taj Mahal

India Agra • Mughal Heritage

Story

The Taj Mahal stands on the banks of the Yamuna River in Agra. Built in the 17th century during the Mughal era, it is celebrated for its symmetry, refined marble work, and the way light changes across its surface throughout the day.

For visitors, it represents a blend of architecture, craftsmanship, and garden design—where geometry and artistry are used to create a calm, ceremonial experience.

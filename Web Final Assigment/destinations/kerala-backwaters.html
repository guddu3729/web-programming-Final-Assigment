Kerala Backwaters

India Kerala • Waterways

Story

The backwaters of Kerala are a peaceful network of lagoons, canals, and lakes running parallel to the Arabian Sea. Villages, coconut groves, and paddy fields line the waterways, shaping a slow and scenic rhythm of life.

For travelers, it’s an invitation to observe everyday culture—fishing, local markets, and boat journeys—where nature and tradition meet.

Galle Fort Lighthouse

Sri Lanka Galle • Coastal Heritage

Story

Galle’s seafront is lined with historic fort walls and open ocean views. The lighthouse is an instantly recognizable landmark, often visited during walks along the ramparts and the heritage streets inside the fort.

The area blends architecture and coastal atmosphere—ideal for photography, history walks, and sunset views.

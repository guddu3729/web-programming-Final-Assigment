Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple

India Kerala • Living Tradition

Story

Located in Thiruvananthapuram, this temple is renowned for its long-standing devotional practices and distinctive South Indian architectural elements. The towering gateway and intricate details reflect centuries of craft.

Visitors often experience the temple as a cultural space as much as a sacred one—where rituals, music, and local customs continue daily.

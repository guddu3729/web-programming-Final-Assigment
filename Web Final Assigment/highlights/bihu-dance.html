Bihu Dance

India Dance • Folk Tradition

Story

Bihu Dance comes from Assam and is closely tied to the rhythms of nature and farming life. It is energetic, joyful, and community-centered, with drumming and singing that create an uplifting festival atmosphere.

Tourists often experience Bihu during seasonal celebrations, where costumes, music, and movement welcome new beginnings and strengthen social bonds.

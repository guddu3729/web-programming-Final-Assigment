Bharatanatyam

India Dance • Classical Tradition

Story

Bharatanatyam is one of Indias best-known classical dance forms. It is recognized for its expressive storytelling (abhinaya), rhythmic footwork, and a rich vocabulary of hand gestures (mudras) that communicate emotion, myth, and meaning.

For visitors, it offers a beautiful way to experience heritage through movementwhere music, costume, and gesture come together as a living cultural language.

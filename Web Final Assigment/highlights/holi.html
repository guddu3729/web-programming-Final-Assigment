Holi

India Festival • Colors of Spring

Story

Holi is widely known as the festival of colors. It welcomes spring with music, community gatherings, and the joyful play of colored powders. Across regions, it also carries stories of renewal and the triumph of good.

For travelers, Holi is unforgettablea public celebration of togetherness where streets become shared spaces of laughter, dance, and vibrant color.

Sigiriya Lion Rock

Sri Lanka Central Province • Rock Fortress

Story

Sigiriya rises dramatically from the surrounding plains, famous for its ancient fortress, landscaped grounds, and the sense of scale you feel as you climb. The site is also known for remnants of art and engineering that shaped the experience of the rock.

For tourists and students, Sigiriya is an unforgettable example of how geography, architecture, and cultural memory can merge into a single landmark.

Golden Temple

India Amritsar • Sacred Waters

Story

The Golden Temple, also known as Harmandir Sahib, is a major Sikh sacred site in Amritsar. Its reflective pool and luminous central shrine create a peaceful setting that welcomes visitors from every background.

A distinctive cultural experience here is the community kitchen tradition, where meals are prepared and served to all as a symbol of equality and hospitality.

Vesak Poya

Sri Lanka Festival • Lanterns and Giving

Story

Vesak Poya is among Sri Lankas most important Buddhist celebrations. Streets and homes glow with lanterns and light displays, and many people visit temples, listen to teachings, and practice acts of generosity.

For tourists, Vesak is both calm and beautifula festival where the public mood highlights kindness, reflection, and community service.

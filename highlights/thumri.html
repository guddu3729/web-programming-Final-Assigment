Thumri

India Music • Lyrical Tradition

Story

Thumri is a lyrical genre of Hindustani music known for emotion and expressive phrasing. Rather than strict complexity, it focuses on mood, poetry, and the subtle art of interpretation.

For audiences, Thumri feels intimatea musical conversation where a single line of poetry can be colored in many ways through melody and ornamentation.

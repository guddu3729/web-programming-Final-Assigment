Diwali

India Festival • Lights and Renewal

Story

Diwali is celebrated with lamps, prayers, sweets, and gatherings. Across regions, it represents hope, new beginnings, and the idea of light overcoming darkness.

For tourists, Diwali is visually stunninghomes glow with lamps and decorations, streets are lively, and families share food and traditions that welcome prosperity.
